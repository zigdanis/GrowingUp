Widget hint, round 2

Danis's decisions after round 1:

  • The hint is Fable's strip under the mosaic, with × and "Как добавить".
  • It is about the large widgets: the Home Screen and the Today View to its left. It says nothing about the Lock Screen.
  • The Lock Screen keeps only the rectangle (name and age), now with a round photo. The ring is removed (see the end of the page).

1 · The strip

It is already there when the mosaic appears and is never animated in. With 1 or 3 people it takes the free slot as a tile-sized card. With 2 or 4+ people it is a full-width row after the last tile. The little Home Screen in it uses the real medium widget with the user's own people.

2 · The "How to add" sheet

The sheet has two tabs: the Home Screen and the Today View (swipe right from the first Home Screen page). The illustration on top acts out the highlighted step, and the steps light up one after another every 3 s. Tapping a step jumps to it. With Reduce Motion nothing moves: each step shows its own picture. The illustration is SwiftUI, and its widget is the app's real widget view with the user's own people.

4 · Lock Screen widget: rectangle only, with the photo

No hint and no instructions for it. The ring disappears from the widget gallery, and so does the inline widget (the line above the clock). Lock Screen widgets are drawn in iOS's own translucent style, so the photo comes out monochrome like the text, not in colour. That is the system's look and can't be changed. A ring already on someone's Lock Screen goes away or goes blank after the update; this needs a check on a device.

Rules

  • At least one person with "Widget" on, from the second day of use (counted like the rating prompt), after one visit to the viewer.
  • Not shown if a GrowingUp Home Screen or Today widget is already installed (WidgetCenter.getCurrentConfigurations, any system size), checked each time the app comes to the foreground.
  • Not shown while reordering, after an alert or a removal in this session, on the birthday of someone with celebrations on, or within 24 hours of a rating request. No rating request while the strip is visible.
  • Never again after a tap, ×, an installed widget, or 10 launches without a tap.
