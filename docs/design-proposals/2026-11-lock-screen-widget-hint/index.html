Lock Screen widget hint

Many people don't know GrowingUp has a Lock Screen widget. These mockups show four ways to tell them once, quietly, after they have added someone. Fable and Astra (Codex) each proposed concepts independently. Both rejected anything that pops up over the middle of the screen, and both route every variant to the same "How to add" sheet (bottom of the page), because iOS can't add a Lock Screen widget for the user.

A · Strip at the end of the mosaic Fable's pick

A glass row after the last tile, as if the mosaic had one more card. It previews the real Lock Screen widget with the first pinned person's photo, name and age, so it shows the result instead of describing it.

Appears
Already there when the mosaic appears (launch, or the viewer closing). It never slides in.
Tap
Opens the "How to add" sheet. The strip is gone for good.
Dismiss
× fades it out for good. It also disappears once a GrowingUp Lock Screen widget is detected, or after 10 launches without a tap.

For

Against
  • With 4+ people it sits below the fold until the user scrolls.
  • Hidden while reordering.
  • Cheaper fallback: a system TipView without the preview (Astra's variant 2), but it looks foreign among the photos.

B · Quiet line above "Add" Astra's pick

A neutral glass line, 60 pt tall, between the mosaic and the floating button: a Lock Screen glyph, two short lines and ×. No cyan, no animation, no haptic. It is always on screen, whatever the number of people.

Appears
Already in place when the viewer closes back to the mosaic, the first time the rules hold (no delayed insertion).
Tap
Opens the "How to add" sheet. Gone for good.
Dismiss
× retires it, and so does the end of that session even if ignored: one exposure only.

D · Note after saving a person Closest to Danis's idea

The "after a few additions" moment: when the user taps "Done" on a new person with "Widget" on (the 2nd or 3rd person), the edit mode zooms back into the mosaic and a small glass card rises above "Add" for 8 seconds, then sinks back.

Appears
0.5 s after the new tile has landed in the mosaic. Once.
Tap
Opens the "How to add" sheet.
Dismiss
Hides by itself after 8 s, or with ×, or by swiping it down. Never comes back.
Copy

Effort
About 1.5 days, custom, plus the shared sheet.

  • Tied to the user's own action, so it doesn't come out of nowhere.

  • Seen by nearly everyone who adds 2–3 people.
Against
  • It still moves in on its own, which is what Fable and Astra both advise against.
  • Lands where the rating sheet's moment is, so the two must be kept apart.
  • 8 seconds are easy to miss; then it is gone.

Shared: the "How to add" sheet

Every variant opens this sheet. It is drawn in SwiftUI rather than from a screenshot, so it shows the user's own first pinned person on the Lock Screen and stays right in both languages and on every iPhone. The three steps light up one after another, every 2.5 s, and stay still with Reduce Motion. After the hint is gone, the sheet stays reachable from edit mode (a long press on "Widget", or a small "О виджетах" link).

The result: the real widgets (ring and rectangle) under the clock.

Shared rules, whichever variant wins

  • Who: at least one person with "Widget" on and a photo. Open: Fable wants at least 2 people stored; Astra says 1 is enough, so families with one child aren't left out.
  • When: the app has been used on at least 2 different days (counted like the rating prompt), and the user has opened someone in the viewer at least once. Variant D uses the 2nd–3rd person saved instead.
  • Skip: a GrowingUp Lock Screen widget is already installed (WidgetCenter.getCurrentConfigurations, checked on each return to the foreground; a Home Screen widget doesn't count), the lookup failed, the mosaic is reordering, an alert or removal happened this session, or someone with celebrations on has a birthday today.
  • One ask at a time: no rating request within 24 hours of the hint, and none while it is on screen.
  • Never again after a tap, ×, a detected widget, or (A only) 10 launches shown without action. Survives app updates.
  • Analytics: lock_screen_tip_shown, _opened, _dismissed, and whether a Lock Screen widget appears within 7 days of the sheet.
